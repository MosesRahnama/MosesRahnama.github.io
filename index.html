Alternative-Investments ETL & Analytics Pipeline

2026 ETL · Fund Performance · DuckDB

Private-market reports are among the hardest documents in finance to turn into data: every general partner prints a different table, and a number copied into the wrong column is worse than no number at all. This pipeline takes 442 public and FOIA fund reports and produces source-linked facts, a relational fund database, recomputed quality tests, public-market equivalent analysis and bounded portfolio allocations.

Every page is read twice, blind, by two different model families and reconciled by an adjudicator, with mechanical gates that refuse any value whose printed evidence quote does not contain it. Every figure stays one join away from the page, table, row and quote it came from.

Mina Analytics — Real Estate Model (REM)

2023– Co-founder & CEO · Valuation Analytics

A company built with Prof. Charles Calomiris to answer a question the industry answers by instinct: what is a specific building feature actually worth, in this location, at this moment. Research

An ongoing research program on the structural limits of formal reasoning: the recurring situation where a system possesses a property, yet that property cannot be witnessed, oriented, or established by direct reasoning inside the system itself, so that crossing the gap requires a representation shift or an external mechanism. The work studies this boundary in a single terminating system at the floor of computation, and traces the same phenomenon across termination, logical expressibility and incompleteness, the reasoning behavior of frontier AI systems, and confluence. Every formal claim is mechanized and machine-checked in Lean 4, so the barriers are proven rather than asserted.

The Orientation Boundary for Step-Duplicating Recursors: Mechanized Impossibility, Escape, and Certification

arXiv 2512.00081 Lean 4 · Term Rewriting · Termination Analysis

Formalizes the orientation boundary for first-order step-duplicating recursors. Proves that no measure in twelve formalized direct classes can strictly orient the duplicating step uniformly. Results are fully mechanized in Lean 4, constituting the first mechanized object-level barrier theorem for a fixed terminating system across multiple direct measure classes, proved without reductions or undecidability arguments. Also isolates and classifies escape routes within the direct universe.

Lean artifact repository

Operational Inexpressibility at the Step-Duplicating Primitive Recursor Orientation Boundary

arXiv 2604.22844 April 2026 Term Rewriting · Proof Theory · Structural Barriers

Identifies a structural property of term-rewriting proof systems called operational inexpressibility: no derivation depends on a specified input dimension and also constrains the target question. Proves the canonical instance arises at the primitive recursion duplicator, where under any direct whole-term measure the recursor's mass profile coincides with that of a true circular reference. Connects the boundary to the Feferman–Beklemishev reflection family and recovers a structural identity with Gödel 1931 as a specialization.

Lean artifact repository

The Confluence-Preservation Boundary for Diagonal Identity Queries: Non-Left-Linearity, Signature Inexpressibility, and External Guarding

SSRN 10.2139/ssrn.7037939 July 2026 Term Rewriting · Confluence Theory · Structural Barriers

Identifies the Distinction Boundary, the confluence-axis companion to the orientation boundary: a non-left-linear reflexive collapse rule overlapping a totalized difference rule produces a non-joinable diagonal critical pair, the rewriting form of equality reflection. Proves the disequality guard is signature-inexpressible in the KO7 kernel, that the unguarded relation loses confluence uniquely at the closed witness eqW(void, void), and that the guarded relation SafeStep is globally confluent once the guard is imported as an external license. Extends the mechanism to a general diagonal-fork schema and traces its cost-side duality with Operational Inexpressibility.

Lean artifact repository

Failing at the Floor: The Primitive Recursor Test and the Representation-Shift Bottleneck

Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.20034530 May 2026 · v4 LLM Benchmarking · Formal Reasoning

Introduces the Primitive Recursor Test (PRT), a 30-model, 3,120-session benchmark over ten task families, graded on termination verdict, mathematical adequacy, and boundary-admissibility. On the KO7 kernel (TTT2-certified in 0.06 seconds), 58/480 (12.1%) of sessions claimed the system does not terminate, only 1/480 (0.2%) retrieved the correct transformed-call witness, and 368/480 (76.7%) of the answers were outright mathematically false. Establishes a representation-shift bottleneck as a fundamental barrier to direct measure reasoning in frontier LLMs.
