AI-Powered Alternative-Investments ETL & Analytics Pipeline

2026 ETL · Fund Performance · DuckDB

Private-market reports are among the hardest documents in finance to turn into data: every general partner prints a different table, and a number copied into the wrong column is worse than no number at all. This pipeline takes 442 public and FOIA fund reports and produces source-linked facts, a relational fund database, recomputed quality tests, public-market equivalent analysis and bounded portfolio allocations.

Every page is read twice, blind, by two different model families and reconciled by an adjudicator, with mechanical gates that refuse any value whose printed evidence quote does not contain it. Every figure stays one join away from the page, table, row and quote it came from. An interactive dashboard presents the resulting fund performance, benchmark and data-quality results.

Regional Jet-Fuel Spreads Arbitrage During COVID Shutdowns

2020 · 2026 Commodity Modeling · Time Series · Alternative Data

Jet fuel sells for different prices in Los Angeles and on the Gulf Coast. The gap between them is steadier than either price alone, because shipping fuel between the two costs a known amount and that keeps the gap inside a band. I built this when COVID shut air travel down and jet demand fell by 80 percent in a few weeks, and rebuilt it in 2026 as four research notebooks.

Each week the model works out what a gap should be worth from the inventory and refinery figures that were public at the time, and takes a position when the market has moved away from it. Flight data reads regional jet demand four days before the government publishes it.

Three West Coast spreads returned 43.4 cents per gallon a year from 2017 to February 2020, before trading costs. The next step is measuring the break-even cost, which takes bid-offer and depth quotes from a broker.

S-Oil (010950 KS): Institutional Short Pitch

2019 Equity Research · Refining & Petrochemicals

A single-name short thesis on a Korean refiner ahead of IMO 2020, arguing that the market had priced a regulatory windfall the refining system had already adapted to. Built from physical-market evidence, port bunker-fuel sales, inventory positioning, crude quality differentials and turnaround schedules, then carried into segment earnings, relative valuation and a sensitivity grid.

Refining, aromatics and lubes are modeled separately and rolled into an EV/EBITDA target. The 2020 refining-margin call is 6.8 $/b against a 7.9 street print; chemicals are stressed on PX utilization in 5-point steps, each worth about 3% of 2020 EPS. From the 96,100 close on 27 December 2019, the short returned about 21% by 31 January and 31% by 28 February, through the 69,191 target.

Mina Analytics: Real Estate Model (REM)

2023– Co-founder & CEO · Valuation Analytics

A company built with Prof. Charles Calomiris to answer a question the industry answers by instinct: what is a specific building feature actually worth, in this location, at this moment. The model isolates the revenue effect of amenities and green certifications building by building, controlling for hundreds of structural and locational variables. I built the data foundation behind it, an extraction pipeline that assembled roughly three times the building-level feature coverage of the largest commercial data vendor.

I directed the research program and built the data pipeline. Methodology notes and market briefs below are by Charles Calomiris and Jeremy Wu.
